Nimbus Cloud Storage Plan Guide › Cancellation & Refunds

Plans can be cancelled at any time from account settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal charge; access continues until the end of the current billing period. Pro-rated refunds apply only within 14 days of the initial purchase. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
